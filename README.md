# E-commerce-Customer-Analytics-Customer-Segmentation-Conversion-Funnel-Analysis
---
## Executive Summary
Analyzed e-commerce customer, order, and engagement data using Python to identify valuable customer segments, understand purchase behavior, and uncover conversion funnel drop-offs. The overall session-to-purchase conversion rate was 2.41%. Customer segmentation and product-level analysis revealed opportunities to improve targeted marketing, repeat purchases, and inventory planning.

## Business Problem
- Which customer segments contribute the most revenue, and how can they be retained?
- Which customers are less active and need targeted offers to encourage repeat purchases?
- Where do customers drop off during the purchase journey?
- Which marketing channels and products should receive greater attention?
- How can purchase trends support better inventory planning?

## Methodology
1. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**: Analyzed customer demographics, order behavior, product preferences, and marketing channels.
2. **Customer Segmentation**: Used RFM analysis and K-Means clustering to identify valuable, loyal, promising, and at-risk customers.
3. **Conversion Funnel Analysis**: Evaluated the journey from product views to purchases to identify major drop-off points.
4. **Product & Marketing Analysis**: Compared product popularity, repeat purchases, and marketing channel performance.

## Key Results
- **Customer Segmentation**: Identified 359 Champions, 278 High Spending customers, 201 Recent/Promising customers, and 507 At Risk customers.
- **Customer Demographics**: Female customers aged 25–45 showed strong purchasing activity among Champions and High Spending segments.
- **Geographic Insights**: Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane showed promising purchasing activity among Recent/Promising customers. Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Jaipur showed lower purchasing activity among At Risk customers.
- **Repeat Purchases**: Aloe Vera Gel, Face Toner, skincare products, cleansers, and creams were among the products frequently purchased again.
- **Conversion Funnel**: Only 2.41% of sessions resulted in purchases, highlighting opportunities to improve conversion.
- **Marketing Channels**: Organic traffic showed a 5.23% conversion rate, with Instagram and other advertising channels also worth evaluating.

## Business Recommendations
- **Target High-Value Customers**: Focus personalized Instagram ads, relevant product recommendations, and loyalty offers on female customers aged 25–45 and the Champions and High Spending segments.
- **Convert Promising Customers**: Offer sample products, introductory discounts, and skincare bundles to Recent/Promising customers in Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, and Thane to encourage their next purchase.
- **Reactivate At-Risk Customers**: Use personalized reminders, repeat-purchase offers, and recommendations based on previously purchased products to re-engage customers in Kolkata, Hyderabad, and Jaipur.
- **Improve Inventory Planning**: Use historical sales and repeat-purchase trends for Aloe Vera Gel, toner, skincare products, cleansers, and creams to estimate demand, maintain suitable safety stock, and reduce stockouts and excess inventory.
- **Improve Funnel Conversion**: Investigate why customers leave after viewing products or adding items to their carts. Test clearer product information, delivery timelines, shipping offers, and a simpler checkout process.
- **Optimize Marketing Spend**: Evaluate Organic, Instagram, and paid advertising using conversion rate, customer acquisition cost, and revenue per channel. Increase investment only where performance supports it.
- **Promote Popular Categories**: Use product-view and purchase data to improve product recommendations, category promotions, and retargeting for skincare, beauty, home, and fashion products.
